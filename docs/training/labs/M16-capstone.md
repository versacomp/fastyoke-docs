# Lab M16 — Capstone

This lab pairs with **Module M16 — Capstone** in the
[syllabus](/docs/training/syllabus).

## What you'll do

There is no new lab content this week. The capstone is the
final integration project — an end-to-end Tenant Yoke that
exercises every primitive you've touched across Weeks 1–7.

The full project shape, rubric, and submission criteria
live on the canonical capstone doc:

**→ [Capstone — Integration project](/docs/training/capstone)**

## What "done" looks like

You're done with the training when:

- Every rubric category on the capstone meets or exceeds.
- Your Tenant Yoke installs cleanly on a fresh sandbox
  tenant.
- A second learner can run through your project end-to-end
  using only what your Yoke provides.

## Stuck?

The capstone is intentionally open-ended — there's no
solution page. Re-read the [capstone rubric](/docs/training/capstone) and lean on
the labs you've already completed. Every primitive in the
rubric maps back to a lab in Weeks 1–7.

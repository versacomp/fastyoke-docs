# Patient Flow Yoke

The Patient Flow Yoke gives hospital operations teams four connected boards for
moving patients through the building, plus an AI operations assistant that
watches your workflows for bottlenecks.

## What you get

- **Bed Board** — track each bed request from *Requested* through *Assigned*,
  *Occupied*, *Pending Discharge* and *Vacated*.
- **Transport** — dispatch patient moves between units: *Requested → Assigned →
  In Transit → Completed*.
- **Discharge Planning** — coordinate a safe exit: *Initiated → Barriers
  Identified → Placement Secured → Ready → Discharged*.
- **Bed Turnover** — keep environmental services moving: *Dirty → In Progress →
  Inspected → Ready*.
- **Encounter pages** — open any patient to see every bed request, transport, and
  discharge plan for their stay in one place.
- **AI operations assistant** — once installed, your Patient Flow workflows light
  up the operations assistant, which surfaces throughput briefs automatically.
  Included with a 90-day Pro trial.

## Installing

Open **Marketplace**, find **Patient Flow Yoke**, and click **Install**. The app
installs with realistic demo data so every board is populated on first open —
uninstall and reinstall any time to reset it.

## A note on patient data

The demo dataset uses fictional patients. Patient Flow is an operational
logistics tool; connecting it to live patient data from your hospital systems is
handled separately and requires the appropriate agreements with your team.

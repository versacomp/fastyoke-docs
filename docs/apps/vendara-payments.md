# Vendara Payments

Vendara Payments lets you bill your customers through your own Vendara
account, directly from FastYoke.

## Connecting your account

1. Install **Vendara Payments** from the Marketplace.
2. Open **Connectors** under the Yoke section.
3. Paste your Vendara **API key** and **Merchant GUID**, then click
   **Connect Vendara**. We validate the credentials before saving them, and we
   store them encrypted.

## Invoicing from CRM

With **CRM Suite** installed, open the **Payments** tab. Each order and quote
appears in the **Invoiceable** list. Click **Create & send invoice** to create a
Vendara invoice for that record's total and email a payment link to your
customer. The invoice then appears under **Invoices**, where its status updates
automatically as your customer pays.

## What's stored where

Your invoices and customers live in Vendara. FastYoke keeps only a link to each
invoice and its latest payment status, refreshed in the background.

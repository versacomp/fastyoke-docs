# Convention Tour Scheduler

Convention Tour Scheduler is a free Marketplace app for planning the
logistics of a multi-day convention: who is coming, where they are staying,
which tours they join, and which bus carries them.

Install it from the Marketplace on any plan, including the free Hobby tier.

## What you get

Installing the app adds six record types and two guided workflows to your
workspace, plus a small set of demo records so you can explore immediately.

### Record types

- **Guest Groups** — a delegation with a headcount, a home hotel, and tour
  preferences.
- **Guests** — individual attendees, each linked to a group.
- **Hotels** — accommodation options with map coordinates.
- **Tours** — scheduled activities with a capacity and a time window.
- **Assignments** — a single group-to-tour-and-bus assignment.
- **Bus Groups** — pickup buses, each packed for one tour.

### Workflows

- **Scheduling Run** moves a planning cycle through Draft, Queued, Optimizing,
  Proposed, Approved, and Published. You can re-run the optimization step a
  bounded number of times, and record a revision note without leaving the
  Proposed step.
- **Assignment** tracks one assignment through Proposed, Confirmed, and
  Published.

## Running the optimizer

Convention Tour Scheduler includes a built-in scheduler. Open a scheduling run,
queue it, and start the optimization step: the app assigns each guest group to
the convention, a field-service slot, an evening gathering, and their preferred
optional tours, then groups them onto shared hotel pickups by proximity. The
proposed assignments appear for your review, and you approve and publish them
through the workflow. No external service or setup is required.

## Removing the app

Uninstalling removes the app's pages and demo records and retires its
workflows. Records you created after install follow the same cleanup rules
described on each record type's page.

## Who this is for

Warehouse operators (B2B distribution, e-commerce
fulfillment, food and beverage), multi-client 3PL
providers, and transportation managers running 1-20
facilities. You've outgrown a spreadsheet but the
enterprise WMS / TMS stack is overbuilt and overpriced
for your shape. Or you're a 3PL standing up a new client
and want a tenant-isolated platform that scales with the
portfolio.

## Page coming alongside the first warehousing cohort

This vertical page lands in full alongside the first
warehousing / 3PL early-access cohort. The marketplace
apps that power it ship today:

- **Warehouse Management (WMS)** — four-wall ops:
  receive, putaway, pick, pack, ship, cycle count.
- **Transportation Management (TMS)** — parcel and
  multi-carrier shipping with rating, labels, and
  tracking.
- **3PL app** — multi-client warehouses with
  per-warehouse-client billing and audit trails.
- **Free Logistics core** — facilities, bins, zones,
  movements, immutable event log. Free with every
  tenant.

For the architecture and the integration path, see the
[/docs/marketplace](/docs/marketplace) listings while the
full vertical write-up is being prepared with our first
named warehousing customer.

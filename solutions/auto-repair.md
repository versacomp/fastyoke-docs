## Who this is for

Independent auto-tech shops, body shops, motorcycle and
small-engine repair, RV and equipment service, marine
shops, and any repair operation running 2-30 technicians.
Your current stack is some combination of Mitchell 1 /
ALLDATA, an invoicing tool, a parts-distributor portal,
and a paper work order on the technician's bench. You
want one board that holds the work from drop-off to
pickup with the audit trail of who did what.

## Page coming alongside the first auto-repair cohort

This vertical page lands in full alongside the first
auto-repair early-access cohort. The marketplace apps
that power it ship today:

- **Field Service Yoke** — adapted for shop-floor
  workflows: intake → diagnose → estimate → approval →
  repair → QC → handoff.
- **CRM Suite** — customers, vehicles (or equipment),
  service history. Linked to job entities so a service
  writer sees the full history on intake.
- **Inventory** — parts on hand, reorder points, parts
  consumption per job, COGS posting if you flip the
  toggle.
- **Yoke Ledger + GL Pack** — invoicing, payment
  capture, GL posting. Estimate becomes invoice on
  customer approval.
- **Forms — free** — appointment-request forms, online
  estimate-request forms, contact forms.

For the architecture and integration path, see the
[/docs/marketplace](/docs/marketplace) listings while the
full vertical write-up is being prepared.

## The Channel Partner Program

FastYoke's Channel Partner Program lets a partner
organization deliver, customize, and white-label
FastYoke on behalf of multiple customers — branded
operator surfaces, billing rev-share, and a
tenant-fleet management console for running a portfolio
cleanly.

**Pay n Go Systems is our first named Channel Partner.**
If you operate in retail, point-of-sale, or food
service and want a turnkey FastYoke rollout — hardware
selection, in-store deployment, payment integration,
and ongoing support — this is the team to engage.
Learn more at
[www.payngosystems.com](https://www.payngosystems.com/)
or drop a note to
[partners@fastyoke.io](mailto:partners@fastyoke.io)
and we'll route you to the Pay n Go team.

## Where Pay n Go fits

- **Retail.** Multi-location storefronts that need
  unified inventory, sales, and customer flows across
  the chain — without stitching together half a dozen
  SaaS products.
- **Point-of-sale.** Counter and kiosk deployments that
  pair FastYoke's workflow engine with the payment
  hardware and processor relationships Pay n Go already
  brings to the table.
- **Food service.** Quick-serve, casual, and full-service
  restaurants — order capture, kitchen-display routing,
  pickup / delivery state, and the back-of-house
  accounting Yoke Ledger gives every tenant.

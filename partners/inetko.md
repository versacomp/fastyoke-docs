## The Strategic Partner Program

FastYoke's Strategic Partner Program lets a partner
organization provide services on top of your tenant
with **per-tenant consent** as the load-bearing
technical control. A partner engineer reaches your
tenant only while an active consent is in place; the
moment you revoke, access stops on the next request —
no waiting for a session to expire. The roster of
partner engineers, every consent grant, and every
revocation are auditable via the access-review export.

**iNetko is our first named Strategic Partner.** If
you'd like hands-on help building, supporting, and
operating your FastYoke deployment, this is the team to
engage. Learn more at
[www.inetko.com](https://www.inetko.com/) or drop a note
to [partners@fastyoke.io](mailto:partners@fastyoke.io)
and we'll route you to the iNetko team.

The [training syllabus](/docs/training/syllabus) is our recommended onboarding curriculum for Strategic Partner engineers — an 8-week self-paced sweep of every FastYoke platform pillar.

## What iNetko provides

- **Frontline support.** T1 customer-side escalations
  on top of the platform's own support contract. iNetko
  picks up routine operations questions — "how do I do
  X on the platform?", "did my data import fully
  complete?", "why did this transition fail?" — so your
  team doesn't have to grep the docs first.
- **Sales engineering.** A pre-sales technical resource
  for evaluations. iNetko joins discovery calls,
  sketches reference architectures, and runs
  proof-of-concept builds against your real workload
  before you commit to a tier.
- **Implementation.** Hands-on build-out from day one.
  Schema authoring, FSM design, form composition,
  integration wiring — whatever you'd otherwise learn
  from the docs, iNetko can build alongside you or for
  you.
- **Consulting.** Ongoing advisory once you're live.
  Capacity planning, workflow optimization, periodic
  architectural reviews. Standing time on retainer for
  your operator team.

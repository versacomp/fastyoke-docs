## A note on the &trade; and &reg; symbols

The `&reg;` symbol is used where we believe the mark is federally
registered in at least one major jurisdiction. The `&trade;` symbol
is used for marks claimed by their owner where we have not verified
registration. We default to `&trade;` when uncertain. The presence or
absence of either symbol on this page does not affect the underlying
rights of the mark holder.

Trust

#title
What we won't do

#default

- **No autonomous writes.** Yoke briefs land in an inbox; you approve before schemas, forms, or rules change.
- **On-VM embeddings for Yoker retrieval.** Tenant record chunks are embedded on your VM — not sent to an external provider for search.
- **External LLMs are optional and scrubbed.** AI Designer and synthesis paths route through configured providers with PII scrubbing by default; retrieval stays local.
- **Conservative predictions.** Yoke briefs under-promise impact; the platform measures actuals after the prediction window closes.

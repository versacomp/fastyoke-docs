## How early access works

- **Submit the form.** Tell us what you're trying to build and roughly when you need to be live. The fields are short.
- **We follow up within two business days.** If your use case fits an open cohort, we'll book a 20-minute scoping call and provision an early-access tenant.
- **You stay in your tenant after GA.** Early-access tenants migrate cleanly into the GA pricing — no data export, no re-onboarding.

We're prioritising operators with a clear workflow already in motion (manual ops, spreadsheets, a half-finished internal tool) over green-field exploration. If that's you, the form below is the right place to start.

## What you'll see inside

A working FastYoke tenant with the **free Logistics core**, **CRM Suite**, **Inventory**, **Accounting**, **Project Tracker**, **Field Service**, and **Forms** apps installable in minutes. The newly-shipped **Warehouse Management** app is available immediately for early-access tenants.

Customise without code (rename fields, add a workflow step, swap a form), or extend with the typed SDK. Your data + your compiled rules export cleanly at any time — no lock-in, even in early access.

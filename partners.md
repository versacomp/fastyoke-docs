## Two partner tracks

FastYoke runs two named partner tracks side by side because
the work is shaped differently:

- **Strategic Partners** provide implementation, sales
  engineering, frontline support, and consulting for teams
  that want hands-on help getting to production. Per-tenant
  consent is the load-bearing technical control: a partner
  engineer reaches your tenant only while an active consent
  is in place, and revocation stops access on the next
  request.
- **Channel Partners** white-label, resell, or implement
  FastYoke on behalf of multiple customers in a specific
  vertical. Branded operator surfaces, billing rev-share,
  and a tenant-fleet management console are the substrate.

If you're a partner interested in joining either track,
visit the [Channel Partners &amp; ISVs](/channel-partners)
page or email
[partners@fastyoke.io](mailto:partners@fastyoke.io).

## Become a partner

If you ship vertical SaaS to enterprise customers, run a
delivery practice that white-labels SaaS on behalf of
multiple customers, or operate in a specific vertical and
want a turnkey portfolio motion — we'd like to talk.

The [Channel Partners &amp; ISVs](/channel-partners) page
covers both tracks in detail. The
[Strategic Partner Program](/partners/inetko) page
explains the consent + audit infrastructure that makes
implementation services accountable.

Email [partners@fastyoke.io](mailto:partners@fastyoke.io)
to start a conversation.
